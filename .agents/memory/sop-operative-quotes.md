name: SOP operative quotation grounding
description: Why exact excerpt selection needs a source-specific application audit and visible issue-completeness checks.
---

Use source-local, explicitly numbered excerpt choices for model-selected operative quotes, retain exact-substring verification, and audit each explanation against its own selected quote.

**Why:** Free-form model quotes were abbreviated with ellipses despite verbatim instructions. Exact excerpt selection alone also produced an unrelated exact quote paired with an explanation drawn from other text in the chunk. A whole-chunk audit did not catch that mismatch.

**How to apply:** Surrounding passages may supply conditions and context, but must not substitute for the selected operative language when auditing a citation-specific explanation. An approximate quotation must never be repaired into verified support.

Check issue completeness against visible conclusions and explanations, not hidden source-chunk text. Keep an unsupported requested facet explicitly unresolved.

**Why:** An eval initially accepted default-rate-only reasoning because the hidden source chunk contained Note-rate terminology. Structural pass/fail checks alone did not establish that the visible answer addressed the requested framing.

**How to apply:** Preserve missing-fact qualifications and source-dependent, fail-closed applications. Do not force the expected legal outcome merely to make an eval pass.
