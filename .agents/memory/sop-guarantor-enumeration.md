---
name: SOP guarantor enumeration
description: General rules for source-grounded guarantor row coverage and unresolved output.
---

Guarantor output must be generated by code-level iteration over extracted parties, capacities, and admitted obligation-imposing provisions. A tuple with no matching provision remains an explicit unresolved row; cardinality obligations such as “at least one” or “each loan” do not become obligations for every party.

**Why:** Model-generated rows dropped unmatched capacities and treated broad guaranty requirements or loan-level obligations as direct party obligations.

**How to apply:** Sentence-scope explicit obligation filtering, evaluate numeric conditions against the extracted ownership fact, match source subject/capacity vocabulary, and preserve every unmatched party-capacity tuple without a citation.
