---
name: SOP issue relevance
description: How to prevent a supported proposition for one issue from answering a neighboring issue in a compound SOP question.
---

The final relevance decision must require a claim to answer both the validated source-derived policy issue and the requested discrete sub-question. A source-derived issue is not sufficient by itself because retrieval can return a related provision and a planner can drift toward that provision.

**Why:** A compound question about a supported guaranty issue and an unsupported market-rate issue intermittently admitted the guaranty conclusion for both planner outputs when the audit saw only the source-derived issue.

**How to apply:** Preserve the retrieval-seed sub-question on every candidate and include it in citation/relevance auditing alongside the source clauses. Reject adjacent-issue claims even when their citations are valid.
